## Summary

List the key conclusions after the article is complete.

## The problem

Define the workload, constraints, terminology, and assumptions.

## Mental model

Build the mechanism from first principles. Include original diagrams where they clarify control flow, data flow, memory, or topology.

## Performance model

Show memory, compute, communication, latency, throughput, or cost calculations. State units and assumptions.

## Implementation

Provide a minimal reproducible setup, relevant configuration, and commands.

## Experiment

Record:

- hardware and topology;
- model and dataset;
- software and driver versions;
- exact workload distribution;
- warmup and measurement procedure;
- raw metrics and percentiles; and
- known limitations.

## Trade-offs

Compare alternatives in a table. Explain when the recommendation changes.

## Decision checklist
